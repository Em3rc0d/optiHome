# BRAINSTORMING

## Product thesis

OptiHome should feel like a useful optical product first and a visual demo second.

The experience is built around one connected sequence:

1. **Explora** — discover styles without pressure.
2. **Pruébate** — move from product inspection to self-visualization.
3. **Coordina** — confirm coverage, availability and schedule with a person.

## Chosen direction

A spatial 2.5D system using existing photoreal WebP assets, spring-based pointer response, scroll-linked storytelling, cinematic frame transitions and tactile catalog interactions.

## Rejected directions

- Full-site WebGL.
- Scroll-jacking.
- Eight simultaneous 3D canvases.
- Fake 3D produced by treating a flat WebP as real geometry.
- Motion that hides information or becomes required for comprehension.
