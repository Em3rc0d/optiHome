# DESIGN

## Provenance

- **OFFICIAL:** current OptiHome palette, copy intent, WhatsApp conversion path and certified WebP frame assets.
- **OBSERVED:** existing home was sectionally clean but visually discontinuous.
- **INFERRED:** greater product scale and continuity should improve perceived quality without changing the service model.
- **INSPIRED:** editorial product showrooms, shared-element motion and spatial card interactions.
- **GENERATED:** MK0.4 motion choreography and OpticalStory implementation.

## Experience map

`Hero spatial product → OpticalStory → Journey chooser → Process timeline → Digital showroom → Try-on → CTA`

## Interaction rules

- Pointer tilt: desktop/high capability only.
- Touch: no hover assumptions.
- Scroll: native user-controlled scrolling.
- Product transition: 300–450 ms class.
- Reduced motion: complete static equivalent.
- Every CTA and semantic heading remains visible without animation.

## Visual hierarchy

The product gains scale in Hero and Showroom. Explanatory content keeps the existing high-contrast white/surface-soft system. A single dark OpticalStory section creates a deliberate narrative break without changing brand identity.
