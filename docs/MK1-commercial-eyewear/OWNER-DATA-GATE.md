# MK1 — Owner Data Gate

Status: OPEN
Purpose: prevent fabricated commercial information.

The following information must be confirmed by the OptiHome owner before it is published as fact.

## Business identity

- Official logo / brand assets.
- Exact legal or commercial business name, if different from OptiHome.
- Official domain preference.
- Public email, if any.
- Public phone / WhatsApp confirmation.
- Social profiles that should be linked.

## Service

- Exact service offered during a home visit.
- Who performs the evaluation and the correct professional title.
- Whether prescription/measurement is included.
- Minimum customer age, if any.
- Duration of visit.
- Geographic coverage.
- Days/hours of attention.
- Whether same-day / scheduled / urgent attention exists.
- What the customer should prepare before the visit.

## Product

- Which frames in the current repository are actually sold.
- Real product names/SKUs.
- Real brands.
- Real materials.
- Real colors.
- Real dimensions.
- Prices, if they should be public.
- Stock model: live stock / consult only / made to order.
- Whether lenses are included or quoted separately.
- Lens options actually sold.
- Prescription flow.
- Delivery/pickup flow.
- Payment methods.

## Trust / policy

- Warranty terms.
- Change/return policy.
- Delivery times.
- Real testimonials, if available.
- Years in business, only if owner wants this public and can confirm it.
- Credentials, licenses, certifications, memberships, or partnerships.
- Privacy/legal information required for the business.

## Media

Preferred:
- official logo;
- 6–12 real frame/product photos;
- 3–6 real lifestyle/service photos;
- owner/team/service professional photo if appropriate;
- packaging/case/delivery photo if available.

## Gate rule

Any missing item may remain absent from MK1.

It must never be replaced with invented:
- prices;
- promotions;
- stock;
- brands;
- reviews;
- credentials;
- guarantees;
- service coverage;
- customer counts;
- years of experience.

Placeholders may exist only in internal documentation, never on the public site.
